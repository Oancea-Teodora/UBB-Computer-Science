The following scenario is given:

We have a power generator that generates different amount of power,
depending on the fuels its fed, however along power the generator also gais
hear depending on the fule its fed.

The generator has 2 types of pumps attached to it, that can pump either
fule to generate power or coolant to cool down the generate power.

At the start of the process the generator sits at room temperature.

The pumps that feed fule to the generator cannot feed more than a set ammount
in one go and each has it onw capacity and fule type, that  will determin the
power output of the generator and the mount of heat gain per unit of fule
fed.

The generator can only be fed fule by one pump at the time.
Once the generator reaches a critucal temperature or above all pumps feeding
fule will stop and the pumps feeding coolant will start.

Each coolant pump has its own capacity and they each can feed a maximum amount
of coolant in one go, however they all use the same coolant, which determines
the temperature reduction of the generator per unit of coolant feed.

The generator can only be feed coolant by one pump at the time.

Once the coolant pupms start they will keep cooling the generator unti the
temperature of the generator gets below the room temperature.

Once the generator gets back to the room temperature or below the coolant pumps
will stop and the fule pumps will star again.

This process will repeat until either all the fule pupms are empty or all
the coolant pumps fail to bring the generator below room temepature.

Write a c program that simulates the above scenario using threads as
follows:
 * each pump is simulated by a thread

  * each thread receives the following data as aruments:
     > the fule capacity
     > amount of fule it ca feed to the generator in one go
     > fule specifications
        > amount of heat and power generated per unit of fule in case of fule pumps
        > amount of heat reduction per unit of fule in case of coolant pumps

  * all the input data is given as command line arguments as follows:

    ./program RT CT NFP NCP { [ FC FF PG HG] NFP times } { [FC FF HR ] NCP times

    RT  = room temperature
    CT  = generator critical temperature
    NFP = number of fule pumps
    NCP = number of coolant pumps
    FC  = fule capacity
    FF  = amount of fule it can feed in one go
    PG  = power generated per unit of fule
    HG  = heat gain per unit of fule
    HR  = heat reduction per unit of fule
 * at the end of the cicles each fule pump will display a report showing the
amount of power generated by set pump and its remining fule reserv, if any


Notes:
 * the program must be compiled using the gcc -Wall -g command without
displaying any warinngs or syntax errors
 * all access to sensible memory segment must be consistently and optimally
syncronised
 * all syncronisation mechanisms must be properly initialised and cleared
 * during the execution of the program all threads will display messages on
the standard output so we can follow the execution of set threads
 * no memory leacks and/or runtime errors/charhes are allowed.
